# Prelimina 0.3.0

Sharper wetting and surface tension, more accurate walls, and steadier moving bodies

## Changes

- FLIP contact angles now default to a new automatic choice that uses the sharp wall-distance closure wherever the scene supports it and the previous force model otherwise; the choice and its reason are logged at startup.
- The sharp FLIP contact-angle closure now supports dynamic contact angles, including slip length, advancing and receding line friction, and hysteresis smoothing, and measures the angle against a smooth wall normal so faceted container meshes no longer cause drift.
- FLIP free surfaces now keep a particle level set, so still drops and resting liquids stay calmer under surface tension and the surface survives restarts.
- FLIP surface tension can now run at timesteps above the capillary limit on free surfaces by filtering the interface curvature; steps above that limit are rejected for scenes with bodies or contact angles, where they are not stable.
- FLIP viscous flow along no-slip walls no longer loses momentum to pressure-projection slip, which removes a timestep-dependent flow deficit in pressure-driven channels and pumps, for Newtonian and non-Newtonian fluids.
- FLIP Newtonian viscosity now uses the wall-aware cut-cell strain, and the wall shear stress output is now a local traction suitable for wall shear and oscillatory shear maps.
- FLIP geometric multigrid pressure solving is now a production option, and the automatic choice continues to select it on large grids.
- FLIP viscous simulations near walls are more robust, including near-tie wall samples and steps after the density projection moves particles.
- FVM moving-body cut cells now merge in groups, so clusters of thin slivers no longer stay unmerged or form stretched cells; any cells left unmerged are reported at startup.
- FVM moving-body drag is less noisy and no longer biased low, because newly cut cells now take full wall friction immediately.
- FVM rotating reference frames are now listed as a supported capability.
- FVM multigrid pressure solving now works on periodic domains instead of falling back to a much slower solver.
- FVM sealed pressure solves around static bodies, cut-face advection, wall loads, and two-phase interface pressure are more accurate.
- FVM moving-body scenes handle sampled cut cells more robustly and limit each step so a body moves at most half a cell between cut-cell updates.
- GPU initialization keeps scene geometry watertight under single-precision rounding.
- Vulkan FLIP viscous simulations no longer produce rare nondeterministic wall-strain errors on some drivers.
- Held cubic keyframes now start and stop at rest, so prescribed motion has no velocity jump at its first and last keys.
- Saving a scene now preserves prescribed rotation motion.
- FVM edge cases no longer access invalid GPU memory.
- The setup option fvm.mesh.cut_cell_age_ramp_steps has been removed; delete it from existing projects. Two held keyframes now ease in and out instead of moving linearly.

Breaking changes: existing projects or workflows may need changes before use.

## Requirements and limitations

These binaries are unsigned. A Vulkan-capable GPU and vendor driver are required. CUDA and macOS packages are not included. Back up projects and keep them outside the installation directory.
