# Prelimina 0.2.9

Smoother animation and faster fluid simulations

## Changes

- Looping animation timelines now join smoothly when their endpoints match, and motion channels can interpolate file-driven values and rotation speed.
- Cubic keyframes without authored tangents now move smoothly without overshooting, including rotation keyframes.
- The animation editor now shows the slopes used by cubic keyframes.
- Long animation timelines evaluate faster, and CSV timelines now use cubic interpolation by default.
- FVM mesh builds no longer fail on large grids that exceed a device's per-dispatch limit.
- Thin cut-cell slivers now initialize correctly, and seed initialization runs faster for fully filled moving-body scenes.
- FVM connected-component processing runs faster on large grids.
- FVM pressure solves run faster, with multigrid reaching deeper levels and a practical default stopping tolerance.
- Pressure solves with roundoff-sized residuals no longer stall during reliable restarts.
- FVM pressure and component calculations on Vulkan no longer lose small contributions during reductions.
- Pressure solvers for fully sealed domains now handle the arbitrary pressure offset consistently, including multigrid PCG.
- FVM multigrid pressure solving now works on two-dimensional and odd-sized grids.
- FLIP liquid inlets no longer push liquid back into a full tank during density projection.
- FLIP domain walls now extend past outlets while particles remain in the simulation.
- FVM cut-cell volumes are now integrated exactly, and moving-body projects must provide paired cut faces.
- You can choose the host-based topology-mask sampler with fvm.mesh.topology_mask_backend; GPU sampling remains the default.
- Experimental time-consistent Rhie-Chow interpolation is available for PISO face velocities, making steady-state results less dependent on the time step.
- FLIP scenes with six-degree-of-freedom bodies can now use geometric-multigrid PCG pressure solving.
- Passive-gas two-phase FLIP scenes can now use multigrid pressure solving, including automatic selection on larger grids.
- FLIP pressure solves now use a time-step-consistent divergence-rate stop by default, with an optional absolute divergence target; set flip.pressure.pcg.divergence_rate_tolerance to 0 to retain residual-only stopping.

Breaking changes: existing projects or workflows may need changes before use.

## Requirements and limitations

These binaries are unsigned. A Vulkan-capable GPU and vendor driver are required. CUDA and macOS packages are not included. Back up projects and keep them outside the installation directory.
