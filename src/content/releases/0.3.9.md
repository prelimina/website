# Prelimina 0.3.9

Steadier flows, faster simulations, richer controls

## Changes

- You can download an available update directly from the application menu.
- You can inspect read-only ADMM diagnostics, see corrected wall shear stress for unregularized yield-stress laws, and get a warning when a moving body is used without density projection.
- Air no longer appears inside single-liquid FLIP flows when walls recede.
- Water initialization and submerged regions beside walls no longer leave unintended air pockets.
- You can choose hybrid split or interleaved ADMM pressure coupling as flow speed changes.
- Viscous FLIP, collision handling, IDP simulations, particle transfers, initialization, thin-sheet interactions, and dual carried-surface simulations run faster.
- The GUI now shows progress while a solver loads.
- FLIP solver startup and Vulkan shader loading are faster.
- PF-FLIP particles relax only beside walls and within their own phase, while liquid-gas repulsion no longer pushes the phases apart as strongly.
- PF-FLIP can now use PBD with liquid and gas particles sorted by density, and interface particles stay on their own side.
- Free-surface viscosity avoids excessive damping as grid resolution changes.
- Cut-cell mesh inlet jets now keep their prescribed speed.
- Wall contact angles below 20 degrees and flow near joined or cut-cell walls behave as intended.
- The implicit-interface gravity lid stays continuous when the flow changes phase.
- Wall damping and the dual orphan rule are opt-in, while capillary-scale splat free surfaces retain default one-cell wall damping.
- You can open a setup using its absolute path.
- Simulations continue when outlet, viscosity, thermal, regularized, or inlet-aligned pressure solves fall short, using the available iterate and warning when appropriate.
- Inlet flows retain their free-surface depth and shape, including with the dual carried-surface mode and semi-implicit surface tension.
- Outlet flow remains stable as the domain fills, with liquid retained in the reservoir and pressure applied only where flow can exit.
- Sealed-liquid density projection behaves more reliably, and FIRM reports momentum-equation pressure and the applied pressure total on adaptive steps.
- Periodic container shells and flows now continue correctly across domain seams.
- Capillary-scale free surfaces show less grid-scale wave damping artifacts.
- FLIP simulations with moving rigid bodies run faster.
- FIRM simulations with viscous solves run faster.
- You can smooth exported surfaces with the export preset, and surface meshing runs faster.
- You can vary body wall temperature and heat flux, vary body acceleration across space, initialize particle temperatures from a field, and enable turbulent thermal diffusivity.
- Sharp PF-FLIP no longer supports mixed face densities; update affected setups to use pure sharp densities.
- Free-surface temporal blending has been removed; remove its setting from affected projects and workflows.
- Each setup now has its own default results folder; update scripts or workflows that expect setups to share one default folder.

Breaking changes: existing projects or workflows may need changes before use.

## Requirements and limitations

These binaries are unsigned. A Vulkan-capable GPU and vendor driver are required. CUDA and macOS packages are not included. Back up projects and keep them outside the installation directory.
