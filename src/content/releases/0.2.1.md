# Prelimina 0.2.1

## Changes

- Release packages now include curated setup examples that you can open from the desktop app’s Open dialog.
- Viewer speed and pressure color scales now account for body rotation and surface motion, and are seeded from physical scales so developing flows remain visible.
- FIRM defaults to improved wall projection and automatic density projection, and supports density projection with the separation constraint.
- FLIP fills enclosed air cells in concave wall corners, keeps particles inside curved containers, and supports positional repair with multiple disjoint containers.
- FVM defaults to conservative momentum transport for resolved MHRIC and enables balanced surface tension by default.
- FLIP, FIRM, and FVM support temperature transport with Boussinesq buoyancy when thermal transport is enabled.
- The native viewer lets you select temperature as a displayed field and reports temperature in kelvins.

Breaking changes: existing projects or workflows may need changes before use. The changed FIRM and FVM defaults can change results for existing projects, so re-run and compare important cases before relying on them.

## Requirements and limitations

These binaries are unsigned. A Vulkan-capable GPU and vendor driver are required. CUDA and macOS packages are not included. Back up projects and keep them outside the installation directory.
