# Prelimina 0.3.7

More reliable fluid and free-surface simulations

## Changes

- Moving tanks can use their own frame of reference, while single-liquid setups can use implicit coupling and moving-domain simulations can connect to world-fixed exterior water.
- Rigid-body coupling can exchange external loads between steps, and enclosed liquids can preserve their volume during coupled simulations.
- Implicit coupling can converge when either configured convergence measure is met, and rejected iterations no longer appear in metrics or FLIP monitors.
- Explicit FLIP coupling with a light structure in water is more stable, with displacement differences and loads handled consistently across subcycled windows.
- Capillary filling on immersed walls responds to wetting, and wall contact angles work more reliably with height-function curvature.
- Curvature estimates behave more reliably near walls and on carried free surfaces, helping droplets and capillary flows avoid spurious motion.
- Capillary free-surface simulations reach the configured pressure tolerance by default, and sealed-liquid pressure and volume behave more consistently.
- FLIP handles thin walls and wall-adjacent velocity samples more reliably, while preserving the first accessible fluid layer on both sides of walls.
- FLIP viscosity and pressure behave more consistently near inlets and pressure outlets, and wall-load reports include viscous and plastic contributions for yield-stress fluids.
- FLIP supports experimental Herschel–Bulkley and bi-viscous Bingham models, with optional yield-field and solver-residual output.
- FVM accepts open space in exact-geometry single-phase starts, initializes thin cut cells more reliably, and handles exact-geometry interface pressure and moving frames more consistently.
- FVM contact angles and interface curvature behave more reliably at wetting walls and around complex interface shapes.
- FVM handles exact-geometry free-surface transport more reliably, including split-PLIC fluxes, cut-cell apertures, and small liquid-volume changes during recutting.
- FVM no longer stalls when exact-geometry split-PLIC fluxes run on Windows Vulkan devices.
- FVM gives clearer failure behavior when a CFL time step becomes too small to advance the simulation.
- FIRM keeps still water steadier when the free surface is tilted against the particle layout and honors moving wall-face velocities.
- FIRM carries the free-surface label through particle births and removals, and adaptive time stepping works with fixed-step free-surface rows.
- FLIP density projection is available on Metal.
- FVM dam-break simulations and FLIP and FIRM solver operations run faster on Metal.
- The GUI sloshing demo now opens a setup that finishes much sooner.
- The exterior-water setting has been renamed from domain.exterior_frame to domain.basin.frame; update existing project files that use the old setting.

## Requirements and limitations

These binaries are unsigned. A Vulkan-capable GPU and vendor driver are required. CUDA and macOS packages are not included. Back up projects and keep them outside the installation directory.
