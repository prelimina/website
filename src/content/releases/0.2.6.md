# Prelimina 0.2.6

More reliable flows and resumable runs

## Changes

- Desktop runs can resume after reopening a document, and CLI runs can use setup-defined checkpoints; Restart still begins at time zero.
- Compatible PF-FLIP setups with surface tension now use sharp interfaces and carried curvature by default, with particle advection selected automatically.
- PF-FLIP interface motion is no longer damped by a PIC band by default, while explicitly configured band widths remain honored.
- Free surfaces no longer act like unintended viscous walls, including when boundary faces have no sampled velocity; PF-FLIP viscosity also includes both phases when their kinematic viscosities match.
- Flows between symmetry planes can slide tangentially instead of sticking to those boundaries.
- Fluid can move through gaps only one cell wide between walls.
- FLIP flows with particles moving one or two cells per step now preserve their motion more faithfully.
- Capillary flows near walls and contact lines no longer pick up spurious curvature effects.
- You can choose an experimental wall-energy contact-angle method for surface-tension simulations.
- Closed-tank PF-FLIP simulations can optionally model compressible air with an experimental option that is off by default.
- CLI setup validation now checks phase-model compatibility against the selected solver, avoiding false rejections from settings for other solvers.
- You can open a support email from the native application’s menu or About window.

## Requirements and limitations

These binaries are unsigned. A Vulkan-capable GPU and vendor driver are required. CUDA and macOS packages are not included. Back up projects and keep them outside the installation directory.
