# Prelimina 0.3.8

More reliable flows and faster workflows

## Changes

- Wall contact lines stay free on cut walls, use a neutral 90-degree fit when no contact angle is set, and can be tuned with a Navier slip length.
- Reservoir outlets keep a calm liquid surface at rest under gravity and continue supplying liquid when flow reverses.
- Open basin outlets now work in a rotating domain frame.
- Moving walls no longer close a jet-root free surface when covering a gas cell or cause abrupt load changes at corners and domain boundaries.
- Liquid faces now account for wall motion as walls approach, recede, uncover faces, or belong to monolithic six-DOF bodies.
- Particle wall repair no longer leaves stray particles near thin, finely meshed walls.
- Free-surface tracking stays consistent when particle repairs move particles, and wall-held markers or stretched cells no longer distort the carried level set.
- Density-projection solid sampling now follows bodies through translation and rotation.
- Free surfaces settle at rest without unwanted drift or particle damping, including cases without gravity or surface tension.
- Density projection remains active with six-DOF bodies, jittered rest sampling, and reservoir, convective, offset, or backflow-allowed pressure outlets.
- Newtonian FLIP behaves consistently near domain-face inlets and pressure outlets, including backflow and total-pressure outlets.
- Pressure solves in closed components no longer reject steps because of a tiny residual imbalance.
- Particle seeding spreads more evenly across interface-cut layers.
- Particles born at inlets now behave like ordinary liquid in free-surface tracking and velocity transfer.
- Closed two-phase simulations now report pressure relative to the bulk gas instead of an arbitrary offset.
- PF-FLIP now supports domain-face inlets and pressure outlets with density projection.
- Two-phase FLIP setups using hydrostatic pressure splitting can now be checkpointed and restarted.
- Surface-tension sloshing no longer develops excessive speeds or damping with hybrid APIC transfer.
- Capillary forces are included in wall loads even on surface faces not owned by a body.
- FIRM and FVM runs can now exchange domain-frame motion and wall loads with an external driver.
- The CLI now reports each recorded frame's output size, write time, and compression share.
- VTK XML result recording runs faster.
- Particle wall-repair and collider-query operations run faster.
- Density-projection solid sampling runs faster for moving bodies.
- PF-FLIP pressure solves run faster.
- Capillary and wall-contact calculations run faster.
- You can edit wave models, particle sources, and monitor lists directly in the GUI instead of editing the setup file.
- Result graphs can zoom the time axis or fit the full run, with readable graph names and source legends; initial liquid is also shown in Simulate.
- Setup previews and domain-face controls better reflect the selected model, and setting editors have more reliable keyboard, duration, and draft-commit behavior.

## Requirements and limitations

These binaries are unsigned. A Vulkan-capable GPU and vendor driver are required. CUDA and macOS packages are not included. Back up projects and keep them outside the installation directory.
