# Prelimina 0.3.1

Steadier flows, clearer previews, faster solves

## Changes

- FIRM pressure solves run faster, especially across repeated steps and multirate passes.
- FLIP adapts its time step to acceleration as well as fluid speed, and no longer imposes a default maximum time step; set dt_max if you want to cap it.
- Resumed runs keep probe measurements together in one file, and existing probe folders can be reused even if their ownership marker is missing.
- Copy buttons in the desktop app now place text on the system clipboard, so you can paste it into other applications.
- Draft previews stay more responsive in large particle scenes.
- Sealed PF-FLIP tanks can optionally model gas ullage with thermodynamics using the experimental flip.thermal.ullage setting.
- FIRM hydrostatic free surfaces stay more stable.
- FLIP free surfaces remain usable near colliders and in inlet or expression-defined scenes, without unsupported samples disrupting the surface.
- Resting free drops no longer pick up spurious whole-drop motion, and interface curvature remains available across the advected surface band.
- The viewer shows the body-surface color range beneath its field selector, and particles and free surfaces retain their selected-field colors where preview data is masked.
- Millimeter-scale bodies display smoother, more consistent surface shading when zoomed in.
- The setup viewer orients the scene using the gravity direction you authored, including z-up scenes.
- FLIP simulations reach the viewport during runs instead of stalling when the viewer publishes particle velocity.
- PF-FLIP transports heat through both liquid and gas phases.
- Sharp-interface PF-FLIP uses the density of the local particle mixture, improving liquid behavior where spray or entrained air is present.
- PF-FLIP now applies density projection by default when compatible; set c_div to keep it off, or enable density projection explicitly with mode on when appropriate.
- FLIP wall-load reports include liquid near the wall and account for wall acceleration, well-balanced gravity, and PF-FLIP.
- Thin baffles and folded sheets now block both flow and particle-to-grid transfers across their surfaces.
- Particle initialization now resolves samples in narrow wedges, small pockets, and corners where a thin baffle meets a tank wall.
- PCG pressure solves on sealed graphs can stop once the residual is within the uncertainty of the formed right-hand side, avoiding unnecessary work.
- Resolved liquid-gas simulations use dynamic pressure by default unless periodic gravity or a pressure outlet requires a hydrostatic reference.
- Resolved gas near sharp, unlabelled density changes behaves more reliably.
- Resolved multiphase pressure corrections better match the pressure gradient at interfaces, including near outlets, cut walls, contact lines, and passive-void surfaces.
- Fixed-count PISO now warns when under-relaxation leaves part of the pressure correction unapplied.
- Windows VTK frame renames recover more reliably when a file is temporarily unavailable.
- OBJ models with shared corners load without expanding every face corner into a separate vertex.
- FLIP particle volume can optionally expand or contract with temperature using the experimental flip.thermal.volume_expansion setting, while particle mass stays fixed.

## Requirements and limitations

These binaries are unsigned. A Vulkan-capable GPU and vendor driver are required. CUDA and macOS packages are not included. Back up projects and keep them outside the installation directory.
