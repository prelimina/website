# Prelimina 0.2.8

More fluid models, tracers, and controls

## Changes

- Create passive tracers in Probes, follow their live paths, and get tracer results from FLIP and FVM, including when implicit viscosity is off.
- Choose additional non-Newtonian fluid models, including regularized Herschel-Bulkley, Giesekus, and linear PTT.
- Inspect rheology convergence and timestep-retry diagnostics in simulation results and the info panel.
- Set temperature-dependent viscosity and shear-rate laws, with local-viscosity dynamic wetting.
- Inspect wall shear stress and accumulated TAWSS and OSI histories in simulation results.
- Color scene bodies by pressure or wall shear stress.
- Opt to halve the timestep and retry numerically failed steps in FLIP and FVM.
- Restart simulations with prescribed moving bodies.
- Run rheology simulations with prescribed deforming walls in FLIP and FVM.
- Run split-PLIC simulations with surface tension on resolved immersed geometry, rotating domains, and calm relaxation zones.
- Choose Auto, 1x, or 2x free-surface detail and use consistent quality for paused frames and exports.
- Run unattended command-line sessions and capture screenshots or VTK frames at intervals.

## Migrating existing setups

- FLIP setups that still contain these removed settings are rejected; delete them: flip.impulse, flip.particles.reseeding, flip.advection.graph_deformation, flip.multiphase.igfm, flip.multiphase.mass_weighted_combine, flip.multiphase.advect_combined, flip.multiphase.velocity_band_blend, flip.density_projection.acknowledge_research and flip.surface_tension.wall_contact_curvature_min_effective_samples.
- The FLIP variant "impulse_gfm" is no longer available; choose another FLIP variant.
- FLIP pressure: flip.pressure.pcg.preconditioner accepts auto, jacobi_polynomial, tile_block_sgs and the new geometric_multigrid. flip.pressure.multigrid and flip.pressure.pcg.additive_coarse_order are removed, and FLIP no longer accepts the jacobi, multigrid or mg_pcg pressure methods. Move a top-level pressure.multigrid section to fvm.pressure.multigrid.
- FLIP runs saved by 0.2.6 cannot be resumed; start them again. FLIP CSV results no longer have graph_deformation columns.
- FVM with immersed bodies now uses exact cut geometry by default where it applies. If your bodies are moved through the API after initialization instead of by a declared motion, set mesh.cut_geometry_mode to "sampled".
- FVM resolved-gas setups on regular geometry that use fvm.multiphase.momentum_transport "velocity", or fvm.predictor.momentum_theta other than 1, now stop at initialization; set fvm.multiphase.alpha_transport to "mhric" to keep the previous behaviour.

Breaking changes: existing projects or workflows may need changes before use.

## Requirements and limitations

These binaries are unsigned. A Vulkan-capable GPU and vendor driver are required. CUDA and macOS packages are not included. Back up projects and keep them outside the installation directory.
